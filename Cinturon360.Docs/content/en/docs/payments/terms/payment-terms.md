## Supported terms

- Net 0 Days
- Net 7 Days
- Net 14 Days
- Net 21 Days
- Net 30 Days
- Net 60 Days
- Net 90 Days

## Impact

- determines invoice due date
- influences grace period enforcement windows
- may impact whether booking issuance is permitted when invoices are overdue
