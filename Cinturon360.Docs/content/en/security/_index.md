## Security at Cinturon360

Security is a core operational responsibility at Cinturon360. We are committed
to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our platform
and customer data.

Security considerations are integrated throughout our development, deployment,
and monitoring processes. We continuously review, test, and improve the platform
to identify and address potential risks.

## Responsible disclosure

We support and encourage responsible security research conducted in good faith.

If you believe you have identified a potential security issue affecting
Cinturon360, we ask that you report it privately and allow reasonable time for
investigation and remediation before any public disclosure.

When reporting a potential issue, please ensure that you:

- Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate customer data
- Do not intentionally disrupt services or availability
- Avoid automated scanning that could degrade platform performance
- Refrain from public disclosure prior to remediation

## Reporting a security concern

Security issues can be reported via email:

**Email:** `security@cinturon360.com`  
**Subject:** `Security Disclosure – Cinturon360`

Please include, where possible:

- A clear description of the issue
- The affected component or surface
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Relevant timestamps, identifiers, or request context

Please **do not include sensitive customer data** in your report.

## Our response process

All reported security concerns are handled through a structured internal process.

We will:

1. Acknowledge receipt of the report
2. Investigate and validate the issue
3. Assess impact and severity
4. Implement remediation or mitigation
5. Communicate status as appropriate

Response timelines may vary depending on complexity and severity.

## Public communication

Where appropriate, Cinturon360 may publish a public **security advisory**
describing the issue at a high level.

Public disclosures are written to balance transparency with customer safety and
do not include sensitive technical details or internal implementation information.

## Scope

This policy applies to the following Cinturon360 assets:

- The hosted Cinturon360 platform
- Public APIs and integrations
- Official client applications and tooling

Third-party services or integrations not operated by Cinturon360 are outside the
scope of this policy.

## Legal safe harbour

Cinturon360 will not pursue legal action against individuals who:

- Act in good faith
- Follow this policy
- Avoid privacy violations and service disruption

This assurance applies only to activities conducted in accordance with this
policy.
